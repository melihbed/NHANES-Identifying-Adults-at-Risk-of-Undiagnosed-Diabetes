# Identifying Adults at Risk of Undiagnosed Diabetes

Dataset: NHANES August 2021–August 2023, published by the CDC’s National Center for Health Statistics.
(https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/search/datapage.aspx?Component=Demographics&Cycle=2021-2023)

## Research Question:

Among U.S. adults who report no previous diabetes diagnosis, can routinely available demographic and health characteristics identify individuals with a diabetes-range HbA1c measurement?
